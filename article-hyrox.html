What is HYROX? Origins, 8 Stations, Rules & Curiosities

Published by Fitness 4 All • Reading time: 4 mins

1. When and How Did HYROX Appear?

HYROX was founded in 2017 in Hamburg, Germany, by Christian Toetzke (an event industry veteran) and Moritz Fürste (a two-time Olympic Hockey Gold Medalist). They noticed that while millions of people train in functional gyms worldwide, there was no standardized global fitness race designed for everyone—combining accessible endurance running with functional workouts inside indoor arenas.

2. The Official Format & The 8 Stations

Every HYROX event globally follows the exact same structure: 8 x 1km runs, with 1 functional exercise station performed between each 1km run loop.

STATION 1

1,000m SkiErg

Full-body endurance test targeting lats, core, and legs.

STATION 2

50m Sled Push

Heavy leg drive challenge across carpet turf.

STATION 3

50m Sled Pull

Posterior chain test pulling heavy loaded sleds with rope.

STATION 4

80m Burpee Broad Jumps

High heart-rate mobility and explosive power test.

STATION 5

1,000m Rowing

Cardiovascular pacing mastery on Concept2 rowers.

STATION 6

200m Farmers Carry

Grip strength and core stability carrying heavy kettlebells.

STATION 7

100m Sandbag Lunges

Unilateral leg strength carrying sandbags on shoulders.

STATION 8

75 - 100 Wall Balls

Final leg & shoulder burner throwing medicine balls at target heights.

3. Key Rules You Must Know

  • Strict Standard Judgement: Every station is judged by referees. For example, Wall Balls require a full squat below parallel, or the repetition doesn't count.
  • The Roxyzone: Athletes move through a central transition arena ("Roxyzone") to enter and exit workout stations between running laps.
  • Universal Standardization: Weights and distances are identical anywhere in the world, allowing athletes to compare global rankings directly.

4. Curiosities & Fun Facts

  • 100% Finish Rate Design: Unlike extreme endurance races, HYROX has no time limit. Around 98% of all participants finish the race.
  • CrossFit vs HYROX: HYROX contains no complex Olympic lifting or gymnastics (like muscle-ups), making it much more accessible for everyday gymgoers.
  • Massive Growth: What started as a single event in Germany now hosts over 100,000 athletes annually across major cities worldwide.
