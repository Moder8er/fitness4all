Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fitness 4 All. This website is a personal fitness blog focused on HYROX, CrossFit, and strength training content. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us.

We embed YouTube videos on our website. Interacting with or playing these embedded videos may store third-party cookies or track viewing metrics directly via YouTube/Google under their respective privacy policies.

4. Your Data Rights

Under European data protection laws (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information or comments you have submitted. To request data removal, please contact us via the Contact page.
